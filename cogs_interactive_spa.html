Core Operating Gifts (COGs)

At the heart of MirrorMinds lies the COGs framework—a dynamic, strengths-based understanding of innate human capabilities that transcends traditional assessments.

COG 01

Security & Stability

Groundedness and resilience. Builds emotional regulation and trust in high-pressure environments.

COG 02

Creativity & Adaptability

Flexibility and innovation. The primary engine for problem-solving and organizational agility.

COG 03

Confidence & Leadership

Self-assurance and initiative. Drives motivation, influence, and decisive action.

COG 04

Communication & Expression

Clarity and active listening. Enhances cross-functional collaboration and belonging.

COG 05

Insight & Strategic Thinking

Vision and synthesis. Enables planning, foresight, and complex system navigation.

COG 06

Empathy & Relationships

Emotional attunement and trust. Supports inclusion and deeply connected teams.
